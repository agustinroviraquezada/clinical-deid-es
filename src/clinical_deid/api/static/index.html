clinical-deid

De-identification of Spanish clinical notes: in-house NER model + LLM for uncertain entities.

Privacy notice

This is an academic demo. Do not paste real patient data.

Your text is processed in memory only and is never stored or logged. Sentences with uncertain entities may be sent to a language model on Amazon Bedrock (EU region). Automatic de-identification can miss personal data: always review the result.

Examples from the MEDDOCAN corpus (Marimon et al., 2019), licensed CC BY 4.0. Their personal data are synthetic.
